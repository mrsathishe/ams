

### **Project Overview: AMS (Apartment Management System)**

1. **Create a Project:**
   Build an **Apartment Management System (AMS)** to manage and track apartment-related expenses and payments.

2. **Frontend (UI):**

   * Use **Next.js** for building the user interface.
   * Design two dashboards: **Admin UI** and **User UI**.

3. **Backend:**

   * Use **Python with FastAPI** for the backend.
   * Use **MongoDB** as the database.

4. **Authentication:**

   * Implement **Single Sign-On (SSO)**.
   * Sign-in or register should **redirect to `http://localhost:3000`**, which is already available.

5. **MongoDB Setup:**

   * Connection string:

   * Database name: `ams`
   * Create necessary collections:

     * `users`
     * `expenses`
     * `payments`
     * `documents`

6. **User Interfaces:**

   * Two types of interfaces:

     * **Admin Dashboard**
     * **User Dashboard**

7. **Admin Features:**

   * Admin can log in and **enter monthly apartment expenses**, such as:

     * **EB (Electricity Bill) amount**
     * **Month and year**
     * **Details of who has paid**, including **name, date, and amount paid**
   * Admin can **view monthly and yearly expenses** through dynamic **charts** (e.g., bar or line charts)

8. **User Features:**

   * Users can log in to:

     * **View apartment expenses**
     * **See their payment status** (paid/unpaid)
     * **View payment date** if paid
     * **View monthly and yearly expense charts** to track apartment spending over time

9. **Document Access:**

   * Users can **view EB bill details** and **download the document** (PDF or image)

10. **Documents Section:**

* Users can access a **"Documents" folder** containing all uploaded files
* Each file should have a **download option**

11. **Charts & Reports:**

* Add **Monthly and Yearly Expense Charts** using libraries like **Chart.js** or **Recharts** in both admin and user dashboards
* Admin can **filter by month/year** and see total spending per category
* Users can **visualize shared apartment expenses** over time for better awareness
